# Changelog

## 0.1.15
First release.
- Shift + Use on an empty cart that is backed up to a tall player-built wall tips it up against the wall. Use on the stowed cart tips it back down onto its wheels.
- Your character plays it out: walks to the cart, crouches, stands, plays the armor animation and gives a thumbs up.
- Checks that the cart fits first and tells you if it does not. A cart with items in it cannot be stowed.
- Settings for the stow key, wall height, ceiling clearance, the lean angle, the tip speed and the thumbs ups.
